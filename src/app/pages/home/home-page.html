Questions

Good to know

Who translates my documents?

A certified translator. Every translation is reviewed and certified by a person before it’s delivered.

My document has several pages or files. Is that OK?

Yes. Upload everything in one order — a multi-page PDF, several photos, or both.

What if my photo isn’t perfect?

Phone photos are fine if the whole page is visible and the text is readable. Avoid glare and cut-off edges.

How is my information protected?

Files are stored privately and are only available to the translator assigned to your order.
